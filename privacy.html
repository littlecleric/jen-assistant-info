Jen Personal Assistant

Privacy, plainly.

What Jen reads, why it is used and where it is handled.

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Who this covers

Jen Personal Assistant is a private assistant operated by Rui for his own connected accounts. It is not a public service. This notice describes the current Google email and calendar integration and this information website.

What Jen accesses

  • The connected Google account’s email address and account identifier.
  • Email messages and related information needed for requested inbox reviews, summaries and reply drafts.
  • Calendar entries and related information needed for appointment reviews and conflict checks.

The Google permissions are read-only. They do not allow Jen to send email, delete messages or create, change or cancel calendar events.

How the information is used

Information is used to perform Rui’s requested assistant tasks. Relevant excerpts may be sent to OpenAI to generate a response. Responses are returned through the private assistant interface, including Telegram.

Jen does not sell Google account data or use it for advertising. The operator does not use it to train a general-purpose AI model. Processing by external providers is subject to their terms and the account’s data settings.

Use and transfer of Google API information must comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Services involved

  • Google: sign-in, permission controls and access to the connected email and calendars.
  • Hostinger: the private server running Jen and storing assistant state.
  • OpenAI: AI processing of context needed for a requested task.
  • Telegram: delivery of private messages and assistant responses.
  • Microsoft OneDrive: requested general work products and general-memory backups in Jen’s app folder.
  • GitHub Pages: hosting of this public information site, if published. GitHub may process standard website request data under its own privacy statement.

Storage and retention

Conversation history and associated tool results may remain on the private server until the operator removes them. Useful approved facts may be retained in general memory. There is not yet an automatic deletion schedule. General-memory backups may remain in OneDrive until removed separately.

Counselling case records and recordings are outside this general memory workflow. Credentials and clinical storage are excluded from general-vault cloud backups. Private account data and credentials are not included in this public website or its source repository.

Control and deletion

Rui can disconnect the app in Google Account connections. This stops future Google access but does not automatically erase information previously retained in conversations, outputs, memory or backups. Those copies must be reviewed and removed separately. Requests or questions can be sent to xuerui1995@gmail.com.

This website

This site has no account registration, payment form, advertising, analytics scripts or embedded third-party media. It uses ordinary links to external services, which have their own privacy practices.

Changes

This notice will be updated when the assistant’s data access or handling changes. The date above identifies the latest version.
