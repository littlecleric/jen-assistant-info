Private use · No public sign-ups

Keep control

The current Google connection is read-only. It cannot send email, delete messages or change calendar events.

Why Google access is requested

Jen uses the email address to identify the connected account. Gmail read access supports inbox review and draft replies. Calendar read access supports appointment review and conflict checking.

Only accounts Rui connects are used. Access can be withdrawn through Google Account connections.

This website explains the assistant. It does not provide a sign-in form or store email, calendar entries, conversations or credentials.
