Last updated: 5 October 2026

Private use

Jen Personal Assistant is operated for Rui’s own use. This information site does not offer public registration, paid subscriptions or access to the assistant.

Permission and actions

Only accounts explicitly connected by Rui are used. The current Google integration is read-only. Drafts and proposed actions need review; connecting an account does not authorise sending email, deleting data, publishing content or changing appointments.

Check important work

AI output can be inaccurate or incomplete. Rui reviews facts, sources, wording, recipients and dates before relying on or sharing important work. Jen is not an emergency service and does not replace professional judgement.

Availability

Jen depends on the server, internet connection and external service providers. Access can be interrupted by outages, expired sign-ins, usage limits or provider changes. There is no guarantee of uninterrupted operation.

Connected services

Use of Google, OpenAI, Telegram, Microsoft and hosting services remains subject to their applicable terms and settings. More information about data handling is in the privacy notice.

Contact and changes

Questions can be sent to xuerui1995@gmail.com. These use details may change as the assistant develops; the latest date appears above.
